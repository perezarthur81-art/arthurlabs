In short

  • What you enter in our apps is stored only on your device. Arthur Labs does not receive it and cannot see it.
  • There are no user accounts or passwords, and we never ask you to connect your bank.
  • We use no cookies, analytics, ads or trackers, and the apps do not send your data to third parties.
  • If you buy Pro, Hotmart shares your name, your email and the status of your purchase with us. Never your card details.
  • We do not sell personal data.

Who is responsible for this policy

Arthur Labs is a trade name registered in the Dominican Republic, based in Santo Domingo. Its owner is the data controller for the data described here. In this text, “we” means Arthur Labs.

This policy applies to the arthurlabs.app site and to our apps: Norte (personal finance), Cuadre Préstamos (loans, installments and collections) and Clavuno (quotes, invoices and payments). Norte also has its own privacy policy, with the same rules explained for that app.

The data you save in the apps

Everything you enter in an app is saved in the browser storage of your own device. For example:

  • Norte: income, expenses, budgets, goals, debts, savings circles and personal loans.
  • Cuadre Préstamos: loans, installments, payments and receipts, plus the client details you choose to enter: name, phone, national ID number, address, reference or guarantor, and notes.
  • Clavuno: your business details and logo, your catalog, quotes, invoices and payments, plus your clients’ details: name, tax ID, phone, email and address.

Your preferences, such as language, currency and theme, are saved too.

That data is not sent to Arthur Labs. We don’t have a copy, we can’t see it and we can’t recover it for you.

Keeping it safe depends on your device: anyone who can unlock it and open your browser can see what you saved. We recommend using a screen lock and, in Cuadre, the app’s PIN.

Data about your own clients

In Cuadre and Clavuno you can save data about other people: your clients and, in Cuadre, also the person you enter as a reference or guarantor. That data stays on your device and we don’t receive it.

You decide what data you save and how you use it. Enter only what you need, have a legitimate reason to keep it, use it only for your business, and follow the data protection laws of your country.

Features that use your device

  • PDFs and receipts. They are created on your device, without being sent to any server.
  • Sharing. When you send a receipt, a quote or a reminder through WhatsApp or another app, the message goes out through that app and is governed by its own terms.
  • Notifications. Clavuno’s reminders are generated by your device. They only appear if you allow them, and you can turn them off at any time.
  • Voice. If you dictate a transaction in Norte, speech recognition is done by your device or your browser’s provider (for example, Google or Apple), not by Arthur Labs.

What we receive when you buy Pro

Purchases are made through Hotmart, which charges and processes the payment. From Hotmart we receive:

  • your name,
  • your email address,
  • the product you bought and the status of the purchase (for example, approved or refunded).

We never receive your card or payment details.

We use that data to send you your activation key, answer your questions and handle refunds. We do this because it is necessary to fulfill the purchase you made and our legal obligations. We keep it while you hold the license and for as long as tax and accounting laws require. We don’t sell it or use it for advertising.

What Hotmart does with your data is governed by Hotmart’s privacy policy.

Your license key

The Pro key is checked inside the app, on your device. Activating it sends no data to Arthur Labs or to third parties.

Cookies, analytics and third parties

We use no cookies, analytics tools, ads or trackers. The apps make no requests to third-party services: fonts and all other files are served from our own domain.

The site stores a single preference in your browser, the language you chose, to show you pages in that language. It is not a cookie and it is not sent to any server.

The site and the apps are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). As with any website, that service may log technical connection data, such as the IP address and the date, in order to operate and protect itself. We do not use those logs to identify you or to track what you do.

When you write to us

If you write to us at hola@arthurlabs.app, we use your address and your message only to reply and resolve your question, because we have a legitimate interest in helping you. We keep the conversation for up to two years after your last message, so we can follow up if you write again.

Backups

Each app lets you download a backup file and restore it later, on the same device or another one. That file contains your data: keep it somewhere safe and treat it as a private document.

In Cuadre and Clavuno, the backup does not include your license key: if you switch devices, enter it again. In Norte, the backup does include the key and activates Pro when you restore it, so don’t share that file with anyone.

Making backups is your responsibility. If you clear your browser data, uninstall the app or lose your device without a backup, your data is lost and we can’t recover it.

Your rights

The data in the apps is in your hands: you can view, correct, export and delete it whenever you want, from the app itself or by clearing your browser data.

For your purchase data and your messages, you can ask us for access to them, to correct them or delete them once they are no longer needed, for a copy in a commonly used format, and you can object to a specific use. We respect the rights given to you by the data protection laws that apply to you, such as Law 172-13 of the Dominican Republic, the GDPR if you live in the European Union, or the LGPD if you live in Brazil. To exercise them, write to us at hola@arthurlabs.app from the email address you used to buy. You can also file a complaint with the data protection authority in your country.

Where data is processed

Hotmart, GitHub and our email provider may process data in countries other than yours, with their own safeguards. We only use these services for what this policy describes.

Minors

Our apps are tools for adults and businesses. They are not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from minors.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version here with its date. If an app ever adds a feature that sends data off your device, we will explain it on this page before it becomes available.

Language

This policy is available in Spanish, English, French and Portuguese. If there is any difference between versions, the Spanish version prevails.
