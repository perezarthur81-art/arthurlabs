15-day guarantee

All our purchases have a 15-day guarantee from the date of purchase: Norte Pro, Cuadre Préstamos (app and Excel system) and Clavuno Pro. Within that period you can request a full refund without having to explain why.

How to request it

  1. Through Hotmart: sign in to your Hotmart account with the email you used to buy, open the purchase and use the option to request a refund.
  2. Or write to us: send an email to hola@arthurlabs.app with the email you used to buy and the transaction code (it starts with HP). We will handle it for you.

When your money arrives

Hotmart returns the money to the same payment method you used. How long it takes to show up depends on your payment method and your bank; with a card it can take one or two statements to appear.

What happens to your license

When a purchase is refunded, the corresponding Pro license stops being valid and you lose the right to use Pro. You can keep using the free plan, and your data is not deleted: it stays on your device.

If your purchase included files (such as Cuadre’s Excel system), you no longer have a license to use them and you must delete your copies.

After 15 days

After the guarantee period we don’t issue refunds. If there was a duplicate charge or a billing error, write to us at hola@arthurlabs.app and we’ll review it with Hotmart.

None of this limits the rights your country’s law gives you as a consumer.

Questions?

Write to us at hola@arthurlabs.app. You can also see the terms of use and the support page.
