Frequently asked questions

How do I install the app on my phone?

Android (Chrome): open the app’s link, tap the ⋮ menu and choose “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”.

iPhone (Safari): open the link, tap the Share button and choose “Add to Home Screen”.

Computer (Chrome or Edge): click the install icon in the address bar.

Do I need internet to use it?

Only the first time you open it. After that it works offline, because everything is saved on your device.

I bought Pro. Where is my key?

It arrives at the email address you gave when buying, within 24 hours. Also check your spam and promotions folders. If you can’t find it, write to us from the email you used to buy.

How do I activate Pro?

Open the app and go to Pro. Paste your key into the “License key” field and tap “Activate”. In Norte it’s under Settings → Plans → Activate license. Repeat this on your other devices.

Is my data stored in the cloud?

No. It is stored only on your device and we cannot see it. That is why it is important to download backups.

How do I move my data to another phone?

In the old app, go to Settings and download the backup. Send that file to yourself (by email or WhatsApp). On the new phone, open the app, go to Settings and restore the backup. In Cuadre and Clavuno, enter your Pro key again; in Norte it activates by itself with the backup.

How do I request a refund?

You have a 15-day guarantee. You can request it from your Hotmart account or by writing to us. The details are in the refund policy.

I lost my key or changed my email.

Write to us with the name and email you used to buy and the transaction code, and we will help you recover it.
